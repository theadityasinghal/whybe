# EquiScore AI — 3-Minute Demo Script

---

### Step 1: The Hook (30 Seconds)
> *"Judges, over 450 million hardworking Indians—from Swiggy and Zomato delivery partners to rural Bachat Gat members—are systematically shut out of formal credit. Why? Because traditional credit bureaus only score people who already have loan history. We built **EquiScore AI** to replace bureau reliance with verified, transparent alternative data signals."*

---

### Step 2: The Underwriting Console (60 Seconds)
1. **Load Persona 1 — Urban Gig Worker**:
   > *"Notice our console. Let's load an Urban Gig Worker. They earn ₹28,500 monthly, maintain a 4.8 gig platform rating, and process 85 UPI payments a month with only a 2% failure rate."*
2. **Explain the Real-Time Score**:
   > *"Instantly, the model scores them at 850 (Low Risk). Notice the TreeSHAP explainability layer below: instead of a black box, it attributes exactly why they scored high—+145 pts for gig earnings stability, +125 pts for on-time rent, and +83 pts for overall financial discipline."*

---

### Step 3: Adversity & Regulatory Explainability (45 Seconds)
1. **Load Persona 3 — High Default Risk**:
   > *"Now watch what happens when an applicant faces stress. Let's load the High Default Risk profile."*
2. **Show the Risk Factors**:
   > *"The score drops to 300 (Needs Review). Crucially, under the RBI Fair Practices Code, lenders cannot simply reject someone without justification. EquiScore immediately highlights the exact Adverse Action drivers: high UPI failure rate (32%), 8 late telecom payments, and 65 days of inactive phone service."*

---

### Step 4: Rigorous Benchmarks & Institutional Feasibility (45 Seconds)
1. **Point to Model Evaluation Metrics Card**:
   > *"Our model isn't just an unvalidated toy. As you can see in our live Evaluation Card:
   > - It achieves a **96.4% ROC-AUC** and **92.6% Macro F1-Score** across rigorous 5-fold stratified cross-validation.
   > - It incorporates a **2.0x asymmetric default penalty**, enabling it to successfully catch 94.8% of actual defaults.
   > - Most importantly, it enforces **mathematical monotonic constraints**: improving your savings or paying bills on time will mathematically never lower your score."*

---

### Step 5: The Closing (15 Seconds)
> *"EquiScore AI bridges the gap between financial inclusion and institutional risk safety. It is compliant, transparent, and ready to deploy with Indian banks and NBFCs. Thank you!"*
